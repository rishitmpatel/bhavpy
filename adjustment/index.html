    Adjusted prices¶

    Getting Indian corporate actions right is the main reason bhavpy exists.

    What gets adjusted¶

    Each corporate action becomes a price factor multiplied into every price before its ex-date. Factors of later events multiply, so the newest prices are never changed and older prices are expressed in today's share terms (relative to the last row returned).

    Action (NSE wording) Factor Example
    Face value split, From Rs 10 To Rs 2 new FV / old FV 0.2
    Consolidation, From Rs 1 To Rs 10 new FV / old FV 10
    Bonus a:b (a new for every b held) b / (a + b) 1:1 → 0.5, 1:2 → 0.667
    Rights a:b @ Premium Rs P TERP / Pcum, TERP = (b·Pcum + a·S)/(a+b), S = P + face value RELIANCE 2020: 0.9906
    Dividend Rs D (only adjust="total") (Pcum − D) / Pcum CRSP-style total return
    Demerger / scheme of arrangement estimated: ex-day price-discovery open / Pcum (close if the open was stale); check="estimated". demerger="ignore" leaves it unadjusted RELIANCE 2023 (Jio Financial): 0.908

    Pcum is the last close before the ex-date. Volumes are scaled by the inverse factor for splits, consolidations and bonuses.

    Where the ratios come from¶

    NSE's corporate-actions feed (the same data as the Corporate Actions page on nseindia.com), cached locally. The free-text subjects ("Fv Splt Frm Rs 10 To Rs 2", "Int Div-Rs.2.50/- Per Share Purpose Revised", ...) are parsed by bhavpy.sources.nse_corpactions.parse_subject, tested on the spellings found in ~9,000 real records from 2010–2026.

    Note: the bhavcopy's own prev_close is not rebased on ex-dates, so it cannot be used to infer adjustments (see research).

    Checks¶

    • Price check. For splits, bonuses and consolidations bhavpy compares the expected factor (all such events on one ex-date multiplied together) with the actual overnight move open(ex-date) / close(day before). If they differ by more than 25%, the record is marked check="mismatch", not applied, and a warning is raised. Pass on_mismatch="apply" to apply it anyway.
    • Gap scan. Overnight moves beyond −23%/+30% that match a common split/bonus ratio within 6% but have no record are reported as kind="unexplained_gap". They are applied only with infer=True — or automatically if the corporate-actions feed could not be reached, so adjustment still works when NSE's site is down.

    Inspect everything¶

    f = bp.factors("RELIANCE", "2005-01-01", adjust="total")
f[["ex_date", "kind", "ratio", "amount", "price_factor", "cumulative_factor", "check", "source"]]

    The same table is attached to every adjusted frame as df.attrs["events"].

    Validated examples (in the unit tests)¶

    Stock Ex-date Action Raw move Adjusted move
    RELIANCE 2017-09-07 bonus 1:1 (legacy file format) −50.3% −0.6%
    RELIANCE 2024-10-28 bonus 1:1 (UDiFF format) −49.8% +0.5%
    SHRIRAMFIN 2025-01-10 split FV 10 → 2 −81.1% −5.3%
    RELIANCE 2020-05-13 rights 1:15 @ ₹1,257 factor 0.9906 (TERP)
    RELIANCE 2026-06-05 dividend ₹6 (total) factor (P−6)/P
    BAJFINANCE 2025-06-16 bonus 4:1 and split FV 2 → 1 on one day ≈ −90% continuous (combined factor 0.1)
    RELIANCE 2023-07-20 Jio Financial demerger −9.2% factor 2,580 / 2,841.85 (price discovery)

    Whole-index checks (python scripts/validate_adjustment.py "NIFTY 500", run on the full 2015–2026 cache on 2026-10-21): across the 499 NIFTY 500 members with data, 340 corporate actions were applied to 211 stocks with zero price-check mismatches; one overnight jump was flagged as an unexplained gap (FORCEMOT 2024-02-14, a genuine +33% results-day move — reported, not applied). The largest adjusted daily moves are all real market events (YESBANK 2020-03-06, RPOWER 2019-02-04, POLICYBZR 2026-09-24, ...).

    For the NIFTY 50 alone: for all 50 NIFTY 50 members, the largest adjusted daily move is the March 2020 COVID crash or a known event-day move (e.g. AXISBANK −28% on 2020-03-23) — no split, bonus or rights date shows up as a jump. The only ex-dates that needed the demerger estimate were ADANIENT 2015-06-03, TMPV (Tata Motors) 2025-10-14, RELIANCE 2023-07-20, ITC 2025-01-06 and HINDUNILVR 2025-12-05.

    The live suite repeats the RELIANCE 2024 check against the real servers every day.
