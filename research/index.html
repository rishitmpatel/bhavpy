    Research notes (checked live on 2026-10-21)¶

    Everything below was checked against the live sites and package indexes on 2026-10-21, not from memory. Re-run pytest -m live tests/live to re-verify the data-source claims; the scheduled CI job does this every day.

    Common pattern: most libraries wrap NSE's live website JSON, which changes often and is bot-protected, and none publishes a test that runs against the real site. That is why "X stopped working" is the most common issue type above.

    Headers: the archive host drops connections from a bare non-browser User-Agent; a browser User-Agent with an honest bhavpy/<version> suffix works. The www.nseindia.com home page answers 403 to scripted clients; bhavpy never needs it, and does not try to get around it. api.bseindia.com also answers 403; bhavpy uses only BSE's download files.

    Other checked sources: the niftyindices.com historical-data POST endpoint returned an error, and the NSE "PR" zip (archives/equities/bhavcopy/pr/PRDDMMYY.zip) is not served for 2008. Neither is needed.

    An important finding for adjustment¶

    The bhavcopy PREVCLOSE / PrvsClsgPric field is not rebased on split/bonus ex-dates. On RELIANCE's 1:1 bonus (ex-date 2017-09-07) the file shows close 818.10 and prev close 1645.40; on the 2024-10-28 bonus it shows 1334.35 and 2655.70. So adjustment cannot be derived from the bhavcopy alone; bhavpy uses NSE's corporate-actions feed for the ratios and checks each one against the actual overnight price move. Dividends are also not reflected in prev close (RELIANCE ex-dividend 2026-06-05: prev close 1303.70 = previous day's close).

    3. Terms of use¶

    • NSE (nseindia.com terms, read 2026-10-21): "User is prohibited to conduct any systematic or automated data collection activities (including scraping, data mining, data extraction and data harvesting) on or in relation to our Website / Mobile Application." It also says content shall not be "copied, modified ... reproduced, ... distributed ... sold". NSE's robots.txt allows / and disallows only /market-data-test.
    • NSE Indices (niftyindices.com terms): "You may not conduct any systematic or automated data collection activities ... without our express written consent" and the site "may be used only ... for personal, non-commercial use only."
    • BSE: the terms pages are JavaScript-only and could not be read by an automated fetch. BSE's published disclaimers say material may not be reproduced or redistributed without prior written permission (example). Treat BSE data with the same restrictions as NSE's.

    What this means for bhavpy and its users. The exchanges' terms restrict automated collection and redistribution. bhavpy is a tool that an individual runs on their own machine; it does not host or redistribute data. It keeps load low (archive files, local cache, 3 requests/s cap, incremental updates), identifies itself in the User-Agent, never bypasses blocks, cookies walls or CAPTCHAs, and stops with a clear error when refused. Users are responsible for complying with the exchanges' terms, including getting permission where required. For commercial use or redistribution, license data from NSE (NSE Data & Analytics) or BSE, or an authorised vendor.

    5. Name¶

    Candidates (all free on PyPI on 2026-10-21; none taken under rishitmpatel on GitHub):

    1. bhavpy — bhav means "price" in Hindi/Gujarati/Marathi and is the root of bhavcopy, the exchange file the library is built on. Short, pronounceable, says what it does to anyone in Indian markets, no exchange trademark in it.
    2. dalalpy — after Dalal Street. Catchy, but evokes BSE specifically and "dalal" also means "broker/middleman".
    3. tejimandi — "bull/bear market" in Hindi. Fun, but long, and a well-known Indian advisory app already uses the name Teji Mandi.

    Pick: bhavpy. (PyPI names bhav and bhavcopy are taken by unrelated small projects; bhavpy is distinct.)
